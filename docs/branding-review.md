# Public naming and branding review

Review date: 2026-09-25

Current name: **Codex Quota Monitor**

Current disclaimer: **Unofficial community tool. Not affiliated with or
endorsed by OpenAI.**

## Published guidance

OpenAI's current [Design Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) state that the
OpenAI name, logos, ChatGPT/GPT brands, and other OpenAI marks belong to OpenAI.
They prohibit misleading affiliation or endorsement, require third-party marks
to be less prominent than the third party's own brand, and state that model
names are not permitted in app titles because they may confuse users. OpenAI's
[App Developer Terms](https://openai.com/policies/developer-apps-terms/) also
prohibit design choices or statements that reasonably imply OpenAI created,
supports, certifies, endorses, or jointly offers a third-party app.

Codex is plainly used by OpenAI as the name of an OpenAI product/service. The
public guidance reviewed here does not expressly say whether the word `Codex`
is treated as a model name for the app-title prohibition or whether a purely
descriptive third-party title such as the current one is permitted. The
disclaimer helps avoid affiliation confusion but does not itself grant
permission to use a mark in a product title.

This is a branding-risk assessment, not a trademark clearance or legal
conclusion.

## Launch gate

Before publication, the owner should do one of the following:

1. obtain written clarification/permission from OpenAI for the current title;
2. obtain legal trademark advice and accept the documented risk; or
3. rename the product before public artifacts establish user recognition.

The app must not use OpenAI logos, the Blossom, OpenAI Sans, or a visual style
that suggests an official OpenAI product without express authorization.

## Lower-risk naming directions

These are brainstorming directions, not cleared names; availability and
trademark searches are still required:

- Quota Window Monitor
- Developer Quota Monitor
- Local Usage Window
- Usage Reset Monitor
- Local Quota Dashboard

A renamed product can accurately say in secondary copy that it works with the
user's Codex CLI, while retaining the unofficial/no-endorsement disclaimer.
No rename is made in milestone 6.20A.
